## Development Methodology

This project follows the complete database design lifecycle:

1. **Requirements Analysis** → Business rules and entities
2. **ERD Design** → Entity-relationship modeling
3. **Relational Mapping** → 7-step conversion algorithm
4. **Normalization** → BCNF compliance verification
5. **SQL Implementation** → DDL, DML, and complex queries
6. **Advanced Programming** → Triggers, procedures, views, transactions
7. **Enterprise Features** → Scalable enterprise functionality
